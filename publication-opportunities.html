Authors of accepted papers are invited to submit full-length manuscripts for consideration in reputed journals associated with iCDEM 2027. Selected papers will undergo peer review before being recommended for publication.

About Publication Opportunities

iCDEM 2027 provides authors with an excellent opportunity to publish high-quality research arising from the conference. Accepted papers presented at the conference may be invited for submission as full-length manuscripts to partner journals, subject to editorial screening and peer review.

All invited manuscripts will undergo the standard review process of the respective journal. Acceptance for conference presentation does not automatically guarantee journal publication.

Authors are encouraged to prepare original, technically sound and high-impact manuscripts in accordance with the journal's author guidelines.

Publication Process

The publication workflow for accepted conference papers.

01
Conference Presentation

Present your accepted paper at iCDEM 2027.

02
Full Paper Invitation

Selected authors will be invited to submit a full manuscript.

03
Peer Review

The manuscript will undergo the journal's peer-review process.

04
Journal Publication

Accepted papers will be published by the partner journal.

Manuscript Preparation

Authors invited for journal submission should prepare their manuscripts according to the journal guidelines.

Original Manuscript

Submit only original, unpublished research work.

Journal Format

Follow the formatting requirements of the selected journal.

Figures & Tables

Ensure all figures and tables are of publication quality.

References

Use the citation style required by the partner journal.

Language

Manuscripts should be written in clear academic English.

Ethics

Authors must follow publication ethics and avoid plagiarism.

Peer Review Process

All invited manuscripts will undergo a rigorous peer-review process before publication.

1
Submission

Authors submit the full manuscript through the journal submission system.

2
Editorial Screening

The editorial office checks scope, formatting and plagiarism.

3
Peer Review

Independent experts evaluate the scientific quality of the manuscript.

4
Final Decision

The journal editor communicates the final publication decision.
