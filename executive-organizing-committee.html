About the Executive Organizing Committee

The Executive Organizing Committee is responsible for the overall planning, coordination and successful execution of the International Conference on Diamond & Emerging Materials: Science and Technology (iCDEM 2027).

The committee provides strategic leadership, oversees the conference programme, coordinates scientific activities, manages logistics and ensures that the conference is conducted according to the highest academic and professional standards.

Working closely with national and international experts, the committee is committed to delivering a world-class conference that promotes collaboration, innovation and excellence in diamond and emerging materials research.
