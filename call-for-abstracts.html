Call for Abstracts

Researchers, scientists, academicians and industry experts are invited to submit original research abstracts for presentation at the International Conference on Diamond & Emerging Materials: Science and Technology (iCDEM² 2027).

Conference: 27–29 January 2027
IIT Madras, Chennai, India
Submit Abstract Important Dates

About Call for Abstracts

The Organizing Committee of iCDEM² 2027 cordially invites researchers, scientists, academicians, industry professionals and students to submit original research abstracts for oral and poster presentations.

Abstracts should present original research aligned with the conference themes and should not have been published previously.

Selected abstracts will be presented during the conference after peer review by the Technical Program Committee.

Conference Research Topics

Authors are invited to submit original research abstracts in the following thematic areas.

Diamond and Carbonaceous Materials

Growth, Synthesis, Doping and Characterisation

Emerging Ultra-Wide Bandgap Materials

Heterogeneous Integration, Devices Physics and Electronic Properties

Diamond for Quantum Technologies

Sensing, Photonics, Computing and Metrology

Diamond-based Thermal Management

Advanced Semiconductor Packaging, Electronics and Microelectronics

Diamond and Carbonaceous Materials

Nuclear Energy and Space Technologies

Diamond Post-Processing Technologies

Laser Processing, Precision Polishing, Surface Functionalization and Defect Engineering

Diamond and DLC Coatings

Tribology, Electrochemistry, Biomedical and Industrial Applications

Modelling, Simulation and AI-Driven Process Monitoring

Diamond and Carbonaceous Material Growth

Ready to Submit Your Abstract?

Authors are invited to submit original research abstracts related to Diamond and Emerging Materials. Please ensure that your submission follows the conference guidelines.

Submission Guidelines

Please read the following guidelines carefully before submitting your abstract.

Language

All abstracts must be written in English.

Abstract Length

Maximum 300 words.

File Format

Upload your abstract in PDF format.

Presentation Type

Oral or Poster Presentation.

Original Work

Only original and unpublished work will be accepted.

Peer Review

All submissions will be reviewed by the Technical Program Committee.
