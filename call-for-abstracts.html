Publication Opportunities

Selected high-quality papers will be considered for publication.

The Technical Program Committee will invite authors of selected accepted abstracts to submit full-length manuscripts for publication in a special issue of a reputed journal in the field of carbon and diamond science dedicated to iCDEM 2027.

Further details regarding the submission and review process will be announced in the coming months.
