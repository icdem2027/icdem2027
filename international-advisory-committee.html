About the International Advisory Committee

The International Advisory Committee consists of internationally recognized experts from universities, research organizations and industry.

The committee provides strategic advice, promotes international collaboration and contributes to the scientific excellence and global visibility of iCDEM 2027.

Their guidance helps strengthen the conference programme, encourages global participation and fosters international research partnerships.
