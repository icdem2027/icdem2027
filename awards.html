iCDEM 2027

Awards & Competition

Recognizing excellence in research presentations, innovation and scientific communication. iCDEM 2027 offers prestigious awards for outstanding Oral Presentations, Poster Presentations, and the Three Minutes Presentation (3MP) Competition.

Conference: 27–29 January 2027
IIT Madras, Chennai, India
Young Scholar Awards 3MP Competition

About Awards & Competition

iCDEM 2027 recognizes excellence in scientific research, innovation and technical presentation through a series of prestigious conference awards.

Awards will be presented to outstanding researchers, young scientists and students based on originality, technical quality, presentation skills and overall impact.

The awards aim to encourage high-quality research, scientific communication and innovation in the field of Diamond and Emerging Materials.

Awards & Competition

iCDEM 2027 recognizes excellence in research presentation and scientific communication.

Young Scholar Awards (YSA)

Total Awards: 8
  • Oral Presentation Awards: 3
  • Poster Presentation Awards: 5
Each Award Includes
  • 🏆 Certificate
  • 🏆 Memento
  • 💰 Prize Money – ₹10,000

3 Minutes Presentation (3MP)

Showcase your research in just 3 Minutes using a maximum of 3 PowerPoint Slides.

Eligibility

Ph.D. and Master's students with research aligned to the themes of iCDEM 2027.

Prizes
🥇 1st Place ₹25,000 + Certificate + Memento
🥈 2nd Place ₹15,000 + Certificate + Memento
⭐ Audience Favourite ₹15,000 + Certificate + Memento

Eligibility & Rules

Please read the eligibility criteria and competition rules carefully.

Eligibility

  • Ph.D. and Master's students are eligible to participate.
  • Research must align with the themes of iCDEM 2027.
  • The participant must be the presenting author.
  • Accepted abstracts are eligible for awards.
  • Conference registration is mandatory.

Competition Rules

  • 3MP presentation duration is strictly limited to 3 minutes.
  • A maximum of three PowerPoint slides is permitted for the 3MP competition.
  • Judging decisions are final.
  • Original research work must be presented.
  • The Organizing Committee reserves the right to modify the award categories if necessary.
